Original documentation, metadata, evaluation tables and research
descriptions created specifically for this repository are released
under CC BY 4.0 unless otherwise indicated.

## Historical photographs

Historical photographs used as research inputs are not redistributed
by default because copyright and ownership conditions may apply.
The repository may provide source metadata and archival references.

## AI-generated 3D outputs

The legal and licensing status of AI-generated 3D models may depend
on the terms applicable to the corresponding AI generation service,
the source material used as input, and applicable law. Each model
should therefore be treated according to the rights statement
accompanying its release.

## Published article

The copyright and reuse conditions of the published article are
determined by the publisher's licence. The publisher's version
should be used as the authoritative source for licensing.

## Third-party material

Third-party materials remain subject to their respective copyright,
licence and usage conditions.

## Important

Do not redistribute archival photographs or other third-party
material merely because they were used in the research experiment.
Check the rights status of each item before adding it to this
repository.
